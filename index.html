StrataA fragrance layering ledger, graded by evidence
Curated crossover pyramids for the layering-curious, not a substitute for patch-testing on your own skin. Family labels follow Michael Edwards' Fragrance Wheel and the Fragrantica and Parfumo groupings, adapted for this collection. Evidence labels reflect a review of Fragrantica, Parfumo and Basenotes reviews, TikTok posts and retail guides, and most of that evidence is anecdotal.
